WANDERLUST: TRIUMPH is a fantasy strategy/management roguelite played in the

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
· A living painted world: the map is the game board, explored region by region.
· Deep management meets roguelite stakes: lose the run, keep the story.
· Built by a single developer through an AI-assisted production pipeline —
  every system, every painted asset, directed and finished by hand.
