OBJECT: r002 is a strong document but three clauses are internally under-determined in ways that will cause real divergence the moment two directors apply it. None are fatal; all are cheap to fix. With those fixed, ACCEPT.

§5 EDIT_ANCHOR_RULE is stated as absolute but its exception is underspecified. "Edits reference the pristine B0 image, never the immediate predecessor — exception: pure lighting pass may chain." Two problems: (i) "pure lighting pass" is not defined against LIGHT_EVENT vs MOOD_PROOF vs DOMINANT_COLOR_FIELDS — a color edit and a light edit will be classified differently by different directors; (ii) if B0 is the edit anchor, then every edit re-attaches B0, which means edit #2 cannot benefit from what edit #1 succeeded at — this directly conflicts with §6's "same-chat near-miss iteration" premise, and with the validated loop in the grounding (which attaches the generated image back). The rule as written is defensible as an anti-self-contamination measure, but it must say what "chaining" means and what "pure lighting" excludes. Fix: define chaining eligibility as "edits that change only LIGHT_EVENT or MOOD_PROOF, not DOMINANT_COLOR_FIELDS or STYLE"; all other edits anchor to B0; any chained edit that shows texture decay triggers §4.7 re-anchor and the next edit re-anchors to B0. Without this, r002's most consequential diagnostic rule is a coin flip in practice.

§4.6 "classify by structural severity, not field name" is the right principle stated without an operational test. For gaze/expression specifically: what makes a localized gaze defect edit-eligible vs a regenerate? The line "structural severity" is the kind of phrase that means the opposite to two reasonable readers. Fix: add a one-line severity test — "edit-eligible iff the defect is confined to a region that can be named as a spatial zone AND the fix does not require changing the subject's silhouette or its relation to the focal hierarchy; otherwise regenerate." That is testable on sight and consistent with §4.7's "defect localized" language.

§3 G7 + §6 re-anchor logging has a residual attribution hole. G7 correctly says re-injection is a "SECOND intervention, logged separately, never claimed as single-field attribution." Good. But §6 says "every edit re-injects register." If every edit contains a re-injection, then every edit is a second intervention, and G7's single-field attribution claim is vacuous — the protocol will never be able to convict a field via edits, only via generation-prompt ablations. That may be acceptable, but r002 doesn't say so. Fix: either (a) state explicitly that field conviction is only available from generation-prompt ablation, and edits are for correction not attribution — which is cleaner and matches §5's bisect logic — or (b) define a minimal edit form that omits re-injection when the edit is not texture-adjacent. I recommend (a); it makes the diagnostic claims in §5 honest.

Minor: §7 HUMAN SCALE_RATIO is correctly demoted to "testable preset" — but the same discipline wasn't applied to §7 CREATURE's "anti-anthropomorphic" and LANDSCAPE's "strongest case" claims. The former is an untested stylistic assertion dressed as a rule; the latter is an untested ranking. Under §0's own "untested hypotheses are labeled as such," these should carry the same [HYPOTHESIS] tag as the token-collision fix. Cosmetic, but §0 is the document's integrity clause — inconsistent labeling undermines it.

No objection to: §2 field order and the FIELD_EXTENT demotion (correct per round-2); §3 G8; §4 compound-failure-first routing and the ROLE_READ branch; §4.7 re-anchor-as-not-edit; §4.8 post-edit regression gate; §6 budget (2 + earned 3rd + separate re-anchor); §6 carryover; §8 acceptance-by-seeing.

Verdict: ACCEPT conditional on fixes 1–3 (4 optional). Fixes 1 and 3 are the load-bearing ones — they determine whether the protocol is diagnostically honest or merely procedurally tidy. If those two clauses are tightened, r002 is defensible as the v5 protocol and I withdraw any further objection.
