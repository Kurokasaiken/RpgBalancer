prompt v4
Using the attached reference image for STYLE and PAINTING TECHNIQUE only (not its content), paint a new fantasy scene.

KEY — WILDERNESS ("Rude Beauty"), the heart of the world.
pillar signature: the land INVITES the journey — a route, a door, a vista that pulls the eye toward attainable-but-untested distance; unexpressed potential made visible.
light-event: blazing white midday sun SLAMS into surfaces — searing highlights, hard raking light.
medium: dry alpine clarity — crystal air, no haze washing color.
scale anchor: a small human-made trace (bell, ribbon, carved mark, offering) against monumental nature.
camera: eye-level, adventurous.

SUBJECT — relational triad (each element with its role):
CREATURE: a great-horned mountain MOUFLON — embodies: unexpressed potential made animal; a recognizable ram made mythic by stillness and a bronze votive-bell on one horn.
PLACE: a shepherd shrine cairn-chapel built into the mountainside, moss and votive ribbons — witnesses: the heart; a small shelter the land grew around.
RELIC: an amber reliquary — leaks: EMPIRE; a pilgrim's old sun-cult medal sealed in glowing amber, left in the niche.

COMPOSITION — VISTA with a dominant near mass: the cairn-shrine anchors the near-middle slightly right of center, meadow foreground, road threading past it toward a luminous valley and peaks, saturated azure sky upper half. Deliberate cinematic shot: clear foreground/midground/background, focal hierarchy, creature gives scale, relic reads as signature detail. MOOD_PROOF: without palette, it still reads as wilderness because a small sheltered creature stands calm in vast inviting land — potential, not power.

STYLE: masterful cinematic oil painting in thick violent palette-knife impasto — gouged paint ridges, ragged edges, visible layers, nothing smooth or polished, no digital smoothing (Jaime Jones, Jeff Easley). Sunlight SLAMS, searing highlights, every plane vibrates, no flat colors, no muted tones.
Palette: sky vivid cyan-turquoise azure at maximum saturation; shadows GLOW saturated teal-emerald; pigments ultramarine, Veronese green, blazing amber, ONE cinnabar; maximum chroma, stained-glass loudness. NOT-line: not pretty-pastoral — weathered and tactile.

HYGIENE: no signature or painted name-marks anywhere including corners, no text, no watermark, no card corners or vignette border — full-bleed edge-to-edge.
