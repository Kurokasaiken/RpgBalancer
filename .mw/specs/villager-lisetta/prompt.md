Prompt canonico (generatori esterni — la compressa SDXL è in coda):

```text
SUBJECT TYPE: Human character — medieval fantasy village portrait.
IDENTITY SENTENCE: A village seamstress who brandishes her oversized tailor's shears like a weapon, measuring the enemy's neckline.
CULTURAL ANCHOR: The weavers' attic of a frontier alpine village — guild of thread and linen.
SIGNATURE HOOK: A pair of oversized tailor's shears held open mid-snip like a beak, a faded ribbon still knotted on one handle.
CONTRADICTION: Fingers trained never to waste a stitch — now slashing air; fierce precisely because she is precise.
FACE: Ruan Jia sculptural rendering — clean, luminous subsurface scattering — sharp eyes of someone who threads a needle first try.
THE HANDS: Slim fingers marked by needle pricks and thimble calluses.
GEAR & PROVENANCE: Measuring tape worn as a sash, pincushion bracelet still on the wrist with pins in it — she left for the quest without taking it off.
GESTURE: Raising the shears in a two-handed guard, chin down, sighting the opponent along the open blades.
PALETTE: Cinnabar thread and ribbon, alabaster linen, iron blades; deep teal shadows. NO grey, NO brown, NO mud.
LIGHTING: Solar Triumph — blinding white light, deep cool teal shadows, prismatic dust motes.
STYLE: Split-rendering — a masterful oil painting; the face rendered hyper-realistic and sculptural (Ruan Jia), no visible brushstrokes on the skin; dress, shears and world in heavy textured oil impasto — thick, violent visible brushstrokes, layers of paint with physical depth (Jaime Jones / Jeff Easley).
MOOD: Wiry defiance + precise fury.
NO LIST: real armor, dagger, assassin look, catalog beauty, T-pose, symmetry, modern clothing, sci-fi, grim, mud, brown, multiple people, text, watermark, signature, logo.
```
