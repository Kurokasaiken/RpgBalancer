# Identity — POI Quest Icon Set

## Silhouette

Round, chiseled relic medallions: a heavy sun-bronze rim around a dark basalt
field, with a small central emblem for each quest type. Scaled for POI markers
on the Gilded Observatory map.

## Materials

- **Rim:** Baroque Sun-Bronze, weathered, nicks and patina.
- **Field:** Basalt Black, radial teal/gold glow from center.
- **Emblem:** Painted or relief motif reflecting the quest subject (gold,
  herbs, blades, ruins, etc.).

## Colors

- Obsidian / Basalt Black for the core field.
- Sun-Bronze and Gold for the rim and highlights.
- Teal accents and prismatic dust.
- No grey, no brown, no mud.

## Style

Painterly digital game asset, impasto brushwork, hand-wrought metal and stone,
centered on obsidian background, no text, no watermark.

## Kill List

No grim, no mud, no symmetry, no flat design, no sci-fi, no cartoon, no 3D
render, no text, no watermark.

## Purpose

Replace the emoji quest icons on Idle Village POIs with material, heavy,
atmosphere-consistent assets.
