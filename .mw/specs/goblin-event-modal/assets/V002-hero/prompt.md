## Master prompt (documentation)

A painted fantasy war totem, central portrait, standing against a vibrant Azure sky.

**Focal Point (the mask):** a triangular goblin mask at the center of the totem. Sculptural, clean planes, fierce slanted eyes, sharp cheekbones, pointed wooden ears or horns. Painted green with ochre and crimson war-stripes. Ruan Jia polish on the mask face — defined but not photoreal.

**Matter (wood, rope, banners):** Jaime Jones thick oil impasto. Rough weathered timber pole wrapped with thick hemp rope. Tattered red and white war banners hang at asymmetric angles. Wooden spear-tips radiate outward. Golden tassels and a few feathers. Heavy paint texture, visible brushstrokes.

**Lighting:** Solar Triumph — brilliant white light from upper left, deep cool Teal shadows. No grey, no brown. Strong rim light on the right edge.

**Background:** Azure sky with soft white clouds, distant jagged mountains in deep teal haze.

**Mood:** Rude beauty, frontier danger, monumental but not grim.

**Kill list enforced:** No skulls, no bones, no mud, no grey, no brown, no symmetry, no sci-fi.

text, watermark, signature, blurry, low quality, cartoon, 3d render, busy background, modern ui, flat design, grey, brown, mud, grim, sci-fi, pipes, wires, exoskeleton, symmetry, skull, bones
