# Design Intent — V002 Hero

## What it is

The central focal image of the Goblin Invasion world-event modal: a goblin war totem raised on a wooden pole under an Azure sky.

## Why it exists

- Communicate the threat before the player reads any text.
- Sell the Prismatic Wanderlust split-rendering: the totem is matter (impasto timber), the mask is the focal icon (sculptural goblin face).
- Establish the Wilderness + Empire clash: rude frontier wood and war banners, touched by Imperial gold and teal shadows.

## Emotional payload

- Tension without grimdark: danger is present, but the palette stays Solar Triumph.
- Monumental, not messy: the totem is a constructed idol, not a pile of bones.

## Gameplay role

- Hero illustration for a non-interactive cinematic modal.
- Must not contain baked UI text, enemy counts, or button labels.
