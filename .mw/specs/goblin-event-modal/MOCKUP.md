## Overview

A vertical 3:4 fantasy strategy-game card:
- carved timber/alpine-stone outer frame;
- top parchment banner with ornamental crest and title area;
- central painted goblin totem on a wooden pole with red/white war banners;
- teal/blue sky with Solar Triumph lighting;
- lower dark parchment/glass panel with a single central arrival medallion;
- one primary CTA button.
