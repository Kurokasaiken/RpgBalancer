# F6 — Le spoglie (`spoglie-di-guerra`) → `spoglie.jpg` — BRIEF v2 (ISLAND)

Beat: la radura dopo lo scontro — il mucchio della refurtiva recuperata
come detail island; il respiro dopo la battaglia. La fiction spostata sul
fagotto/pacco — niente sonagli, niente trappole dipinte.

## Correlazione

- **Archetipo: ISLAND** — cluster compatto iper-dettagliato nel terzo
  basso-centro, aria su 3 lati; la radura intorno è lo spazio del
  silenzio; treeline che chiude il fondo.
- **Shot**: `hold` (contemplazione — la battaglia è finita) → drift
  quasi impercettibile.
- **Ambient**: `glint` su oro/metallo nel mucchio, `dust` che si posa,
  `drift` basso, un `shaft` lento se c'è cielo.
- **Hooks**: A2 hidden object — un oggetto notabile nel mucchio (il
  trofeo); A1 discovery su un dettaglio che parla di storia.
- **Kill list**: niente sonagli/campanelli/oggetti d'allarme, niente
  trappole, niente corpi leggibili o sangue, niente fuoco grande.

## Full prompt

```text
THE SPOILS — painted in heavy oil impasto. A forest clearing at last light after the fight — the quiet weight of victory counted in recovered goods. SUBJECT: a compact pile of reclaimed loot at lower-center — torn sacks spilling grain and coppers, dented cooking ware, a merchant's strongbox cracked open, bundles of trade cloth, a sword with a merchant's mark — every object weathered, ordinary, and heavy with stolen meaning; around it the empty clearing: churned dark earth, scattered leaf litter, the treeline closing the background in deep dusk teal. COMPOSITION: the pile is a compact detail island at lower-center — touching nothing, surrounded by air and shadow on at least three sides; above it the clearing opens to the last band of vibrant azure dusk sky between trunks; the eye lands on the pile first, then wanders the stillness. NO symmetry — the pile leans, spills off-axis. LIGHTING: a single wedge of dying amber light catching the pile's upper edge — everything else in saturated teal shadow; the metal bits catch tiny sharp glints. PALETTE: Wanderlust pigments — ambra last-light on the pile, cinabro on the copper bits, verde Veronese clearing undergrowth, deep teal/smeraldo shadow, ultramarine in the treeline; never grey, never brown, never mud. The frame stays luminous and high-chroma — light wins over darkness, even the deepest shadow is saturated color, never black murk. TECHNIQUE: thick oil impasto, broad palette-knife strokes, Jaime Jones / Jeff Easley tradition — tactile matter on cloth, grain, dented metal; rude beauty — the pile is a still life, not a trophy shot. MOOD: it is over, and this is what it cost to take back — the pile is the ledger. NO people, NO goblins, NO bodies or wounds or blood, NO bells or alarm objects of any kind, NO traps or wires, NO large flames, NO smoke plumes painted, NO readable text/UI/watermarks, NO symmetry, NO grim, NO gore, NO sci-fi, NO flat digital. Ultra-wide cinematic 2.4:1.
```
