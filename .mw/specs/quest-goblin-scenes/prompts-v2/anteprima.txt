Using the attached reference image for STYLE and PAINTING TECHNIQUE only (not its content), paint a new fantasy scene.

ANTEPRIMA — a sun-drenched forest edge hiding a goblin warband, the wood gone quiet. SUBJECT — the threat stays hidden: the forest margin in blazing day, a wall of deep saturated teal shadow behind the first rank of trunks, and inside that shadow scattered pairs of small amber glinting eyes and a few crude spear-tips catching the light; no goblin body reads fully, the warband is implied by how many eyes there are. One bolder lean silhouette presses half-hidden behind a birch trunk at the left edge, only a shape and one greedy hand on the bark, never a readable face. COMPOSITION — the bright meadow fringe and emerald ferns in the lower foreground, the mid band a rank of sunlit trunks (one pale birch, one crimson maple), the treeline behind them dissolving into deep teal shadow where the amber eyes cluster denser toward the center; vibrant azure sky above through the canopy gaps. LIGHTING — Solar Triumph: brilliant white-gold light slams across the meadow and the trunk faces, the shadow wall behind glows in saturated teal-emerald, never grey, never murk; the amber eyes spark like embers inside the cool dark. LIFE — a black-feathered crow bursting upward in alarm; a ruby amanita cluster in the foreground grass; a sapphire jay frozen mid-leap from a branch. PALETTE — vibrant azure sky and Veronese green against deep teal shadow, cinnabar maple and ruby amanita sparks, amber light and amber eyes; chromatic movement inside every field — warm flecks within cool fields, cool glints within warm fields; every plane vibrates. TECHNIQUE — violent palette-knife impasto, visible layers, no digital smoothing, Jaime Jones / Jeff Easley tradition; trunks and near ferns clean-edged where light slams, the shadow mass tactile matter; rude beauty — weathered, never squalid. MOOD — rude beauty watched by hungry eyes; luminous high-chroma, even the deepest shadow is saturated color, never black murk. HYGIENE — NO text, NO letters, NO name-marks, NO borders or rounded corners, NO readable faces or hero figure, NO people, NO camp structures, NO totems, NO symmetry, NO flat digital smoothing, NO grey, NO brown, NO black fields.
