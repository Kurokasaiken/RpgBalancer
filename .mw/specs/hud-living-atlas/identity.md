# HUD Living Atlas — Asset Identity

**Component ID:** `hud-living-atlas`
**Type:** full-screen UI mockup / game frame
**Status:** exploration

---

## Silhouette

A full-bleed horizontal painted map filling the entire frame edge-to-edge. The HUD is a sparse set of small objects anchored to the perimeter: a thin cluster of tiny round medallions docked on the mid-left edge, one small parchment tag hanging near the top-right corner, a minimal row of small nav glyphs centered on the bottom edge, and a tiny day/night marker near top-center. No outer frame, no rails, no panels. The map is ~90% of the composition.

## Materials

- **World:** painterly continent — lush meadows, crystal azure river, golden thatch village, alpine stone peaks, vibrant azure sea and sky. Oil impasto, visible brushwork.
- **Medallions:** small Baroque Sun-Bronze coins (~3% of screen width) with weathered patina and a single bright solar highlight.
- **Objective tag:** warm parchment slip with a thin gold inner stroke, slightly tilted, casting a soft teal shadow onto the map.
- **Nav glyphs:** shallow incised marks on a translucent dark-teal sliver, not filled gold buttons.
- **Shadows:** always deep cool teal/emerald — never grey, never brown.

## Colors

- **Azure world:** #23889A sea/sky
- **Deep teal shadow:** #082E35
- **Sun-Bronze:** #C58A32 (rims only)
- **Solar gold highlight:** #E2BD63 (one focal highlight per object max)
- **Solar white light:** #F4ECD5
- **Parchment:** #E7D3A3 with ink #302516
- **Cinnabar accent:** #B84B38 only if an urgency marker appears

## Kill list (must not contain)

- Text, watermark, signature, readable letters or numbers.
- Opaque top ribbon, solid bottom bar, full-height side rails, second decorative frame, window-inside-window composition.
- Weather icons, mana orbs, crystal resources, health bars, combat meters, notification bells.
- Perfect symmetry, flat digital planes, modern UI chrome, sci-fi elements.
- Grim darkness, mud, grey or brown shadows.

## Purpose

Visual reference for the Living Atlas HUD direction: prove that precision HUD can live on the edge of a full-bleed painted world without dashboard chrome.
