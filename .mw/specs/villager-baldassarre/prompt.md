# Baldassarre — Image Prompt (V2 §10.2 umani, compilato dal Motore di Unicità in identity.md)

Prompt canonico (generatori esterni — la compressa SDXL è in coda):

```text
SUBJECT TYPE: Human character — medieval fantasy village portrait.
IDENTITY SENTENCE: A village butcher who holds the line behind a tavern door because nobody gave him a shield.
CULTURAL ANCHOR: The butcher's shop and tavern of a frontier alpine village.
SIGNATURE HOOK: A wooden tavern door carried on one arm like a tower shield, iron hinges and handle still mounted.
CONTRADICTION: The biggest man in the village — bravery made of pure terror.
FACE: Ruan Jia sculptural rendering — clean, luminous subsurface scattering — wide flushed face, terrified brave eyes peeking over the door's edge.
THE HANDS: Butcher's hands: huge, stained, knuckles that break bones for a living.
GEAR & PROVENANCE: Grease-stained work apron, meat cleaver in the other fist, a boar-tusk necklace — the only dangerous thing he ever faced before.
GESTURE: Leaning his whole weight forward behind the door-shield, in medias res, one eye over the rim.
PALETTE: Basalt-iron hinges and cleaver, cinnabar apron, sun-bleached wood gold; deep teal shadows. NO grey, NO brown, NO mud.
LIGHTING: Solar Triumph — blinding white light, deep cool teal shadows, prismatic dust motes.
STYLE: Split-rendering — a masterful oil painting; the face rendered hyper-realistic and sculptural (Ruan Jia), no visible brushstrokes on the skin; door, apron and world in heavy textured oil impasto — thick, violent visible brushstrokes, layers of paint with physical depth (Jaime Jones / Jeff Easley).
MOOD: Terror held upright + stubborn loyalty.
NO LIST: real shield, real armor, helmet, T-pose, symmetry, modern clothing, sci-fi, grim, mud, brown, multiple people, text, watermark, signature, logo.
```
