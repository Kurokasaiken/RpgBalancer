# D100 Pinball — Asset Identity

**Component ID:** `d100-pinball`
**Type:** UI component / interactive artifact
**Status:** exploration

---

## Silhouette

A circular pinball playfield, 1:1, framed by an asymmetric Baroque Sun-Bronze rim. The center is a dark obsidian board. From the center radiate 5 wedge-shaped or concentric wells (zones), each with a distinct color. A gold marble sits at the launch rail or is streaking through the lanes. Crystal bumpers are placed at the 5 skill axes. Dark indigo tar lanes separate the zones. The whole thing reads like a compact pinball table viewed from above.

## Materials

- **Frame:** Baroque Sun-Bronze (scratched, patina, micro-nicks), asymmetric finials.
- **Playfield:** polished obsidian with teal caustic reflections.
- **Zones:** colored glass/crystal wells — gold (triumph), amber (win), bronze (almost), purple (fail), crimson (epic fail).
- **Lanes:** dark indigo tar, organic edges.
- **Bumpers:** ivory crystal with gold core, asymmetric 5 around the rim.
- **Ball:** molten gold/amber marble with a glowing trail.
- **Launch rail:** curved bronze channel at the top, leading into the playfield.

## Colors

- **Triumph zone:** #fce890 / #ffeebc (bright gold)
- **Win zone:** #e4b048 / #c9a227 (gold)
- **Almost zone:** #b08035 / #8a5a18 (bronze)
- **Fail zone:** #5a6bff / #4a4a7a (indigo/purple)
- **Epic fail zone:** #9e2b20 / #3d0a18 (crimson)
- **Frame:** #c9a227, #8a5a18, #602c08, #2e1a06
- **Playfield shadows:** #0a1216, #02020b, rgba(16,110,95,.35)
- **Ivory text:** #f0efe4
- **Teal accents:** #8db3a5, #10b981
- **No grey, no brown, no mud.**

## Kill list (must not contain)

- Text, watermark, signature.
- Perfect radial symmetry; the frame can be balanced but not mirrored.
- Flat digital surfaces or clean vector gradients only.
- Sci-fi (wires, screens, holograms).
- Grim/dark blood, skulls, decay.
- Modern UI (buttons, sliders, progress bars) inside the playfield.

## Purpose

Show the probability distribution of a D100 skill check as a physical pinball landing. The ball is the roll; the zones are the outcomes.

## Variants needed

- **Empire / gilded-observatory:** heavy bronze, obsidian, prismatic light, saturated zones.
- **Wilderness / rustic-orrery:** lighter brass/timber, slate, azure highlights, softer zones.
