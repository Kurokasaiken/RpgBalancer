Prompt canonico (generatori esterni — la compressa SDXL è in coda):

```text
SUBJECT TYPE: Human character — medieval fantasy village portrait.
IDENTITY SENTENCE: A village master carpenter who marches to battle gripping his work mallet like a warhammer — and is visibly terrified by his own courage.
CULTURAL ANCHOR: The carpenters' workshop of a frontier alpine village — timber-and-thatch builders' guild.
SIGNATURE HOOK: A heavy carpenter's mallet held over the shoulder like a weapon, its handle still wrapped in the string he uses to measure timber.
CONTRADICTION: Hands trained to measure twice and cut once — now swinging without measuring.
FACE: Ruan Jia sculptural rendering — clean, luminous subsurface scattering — sawdust clinging to a greying beard, stubborn nervous eyes.
THE HANDS: Big plane-calloused palms, knuckles swollen from a lifetime at the bench.
GEAR & PROVENANCE: Scorched leather work apron bristling with chisels like a bandolier; a fresh notch carved on the mallet — his one survived quest.
GESTURE: Marching forward, mallet raised on his shoulder, half-looking back as if hoping someone will call him home.
PALETTE: Honey-gold timber and sunlight, cinnabar details, worn iron; deep teal shadows. NO grey, NO brown, NO mud.
LIGHTING: Solar Triumph — blinding white light from above, deep cool teal shadows, prismatic dust motes.
STYLE: Split-rendering — a masterful oil painting; the face rendered hyper-realistic and sculptural (Ruan Jia), no visible brushstrokes on the skin; body, apron, mallet and world in heavy textured oil impasto — thick, violent visible brushstrokes, layers of paint with physical depth (Jaime Jones / Jeff Easley).
MOOD: Stubborn terror + improvised heroism.
NO LIST: real armor, helmet, sword, soldier gear, T-pose, symmetry, modern clothing, sci-fi, grim, mud, brown, multiple people, text, watermark, signature, logo.
```
