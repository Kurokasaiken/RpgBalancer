# Goblin Totem Hero — Identity

## Creature family

Goblin — Tribal / Wilderness Faction.

## Visual DNA

- Mask-like carved face with large, slanted, glowing or cunning eyes.
- Long pointed nose or beak, not human.
- Green war-paint pigment streaks across wood/bone.
- Tattered red banners and dyed straw hanging from the standard.
- Sharpened wooden spears, braided cord, feathers.
- Rough, hand-carved texture; no polished metal, no symmetry.

## Symbolism

- Wild aggressive nature colliding with an ordered heroic world.
- Urgency and clash, not dread or apocalypse.
- A warband standard, not a death icon.

## Silhouette

- Vertical emphasis with horizontal banner/spear arms.
- Triangular top (mask points) with a central nose ridge.
- Tattered cloth and straw breaking the silhouette for kinetic energy.
