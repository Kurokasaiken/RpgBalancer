# Goblin Totem Hero — Design Intent

## Purpose

Generate the central hero artwork for the Goblin Invasion event card. The totem is a crude goblin warband standard — the single AI-generated asset that CSS/SVG cannot reproduce with the required material richness.

## Role in the component

- The totem sits behind or above the event title and above the description panel.
- It is the focal illustration of the card.
- It must not contain text, frames, buttons, or UI.
- It must be usable as a contained or masked image within the React layout.

## Style rules from the Bible

- Prismatic Wanderlust, kinetic, solar, materially rich.
- Split rendering:
  - Totem face/icon: clean, sculptural, Ruan Jia-like.
  - Body/material: thick oil impasto, Jaime Jones/Jeff Easley-like.
- Shadows: deep cool teal, emerald, turquoise — never grey or brown.
- Light: Solar Triumph — brilliant white light from upper left, golden dust, subtle flare.
- Materials: carved wood, bone, knotted rope, feathers, dyed straw, tattered red banners.
- Forbidden: skulls, gore, decay, grimdark, muddy brown, grey fog, perfect symmetry, flat vector, sci-fi, pipes, wires, exoskeletons, photorealistic horror.
