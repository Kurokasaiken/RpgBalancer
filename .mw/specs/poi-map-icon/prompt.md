# Prompt — Icona mappa «Punto d'interesse» (ottone su ottone)

Channel: Gemini image API (`generateContent`, `responseModalities: ["IMAGE"]`),
flat magenta #FF00FF for keying. One image per direction.

Shared style block (kit v1 adapted — the marker IS the subject, so the
«no frame» rule of the emblem kit does not apply):

```text
STYLE — Wanderlust painted map marker. A small ornate brass marker for the
world map of a fantasy strategy game, centred and seen from the front, with a
bold silhouette that fills about 80% of the square; it must read as one solid
shape at 16 pixels and stay distinct in greyscale. MATERIAL: all metal —
hand-cast champagne brass, rubbed warm and bright on every raised edge, with
deeper antique bronze pooled in the hollows; depth comes from engraving and
relief, not enamel colour fields. LIGHT: one warm white key light from the
upper left, deep cool teal shadow on the lower right; a strong value split
between the lit face and the shadow side. RENDERING: painted game asset, rich
oil impasto in the manner of Jaime Jones on the metal surfaces, a crisp clean
silhouette edge, engraved detail kept bold and sparse so it survives at 16
pixels; luminous, never black murk; shadows are deep teal, NO grey, NO muddy
brown.
```

Shared rules:

```text
STRICT RULES: no text, letters, numbers or runes; no skulls or bones; no
scene, no landscape, no ground shadow; no flat vector look, no 3D-render
plastic, no cartoon outline; no purple, no grey, no brown, no mud; no busy
filigree finer than a thick engraved notch.

OUTPUT: square 1:1 image, the marker alone on a perfectly flat solid magenta
background (#FF00FF) with no gradient and no shadow on it; the background
will be keyed out and the marker reduced to 64, 32, 24, 20 and 16 px.
```
