# Goblin Invasion Totem — Asset Identity Card

## Asset Type

UI mockup / event notification panel (vertical card, 3:4 aspect ratio).

## Purpose

A single ornate interface panel that communicates a goblin warband invasion event.

## Silhouette Hook

Vertical card framed by asymmetric hand-carved Timber and Alpine Stone, with a prominent central totem and a top canvas/leather banner.

## Shape Language

- Primary: vertical rectangle with slightly irregular outer frame
- Accent: triangular/diamond Golden Thatch corners, curved horns, jagged feathers
- Asymmetry: left side heavier, right side lighter; top banner off-center

## Materials

- **Frame:** hand-carved Timber, rough-hewn wood grain, Alpine Stone ribs, Golden Thatch woven corners
- **Centerpiece:** crude goblin tribal totem carved from bone and knotted wood, war-paint pigment markings, braided cord, feathers, dyed straw
- **Banner:** aged canvas/leather strip across the top
- **Accents:** Baroque Sun-Bronze ceremonial plates where metal appears

## Colors

- **Primary:** forest green, warm amber, cinnabar red
- **Secondary:** ultramarine blue accents
- **Shadows:** deep cool Teal / Emerald (NO grey, NO brown)
- **Light:** clean white sunlight, Solar Triumph, faint golden dust motes
- **Forbidden:** grey, brown, muddy tones, black fog

## Lighting

Solar Triumph — brilliant clean white sunlight from upper left, deep cool Teal shadows, faint golden dust motes drifting through the light, subtle prismatic lens flare.

## Style

- Thick oil impasto brushstrokes
- Jaime Jones broad painterly technique on every surface
- Split-rendering: clean focal totem / impasto surrounding matter
- Rude Beauty, kinetic energy, wild aggressive nature colliding with ordered heroic world
