# Goblin Invasion Totem — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Notify the player that a hostile goblin warband is approaching the village, creating urgency and a clear call to prepare defenses.

## Desired Emotion

Urgent, wild, aggressive-but-not-dreadful. The panel should feel like a tribal warning, not an apocalyptic prophecy.

## Player Lesson

World events are visually loud and culturally specific; preparation is preferable to panic.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The wilderness is not only beautiful — it is also where aggressive, unruly forces collide with ordered frontier life. This panel dramatizes that clash.

## World Pillar

Wilderness — ancient sun-drenched forests at the edge of the known world, rude beauty, kinetic energy.

## Skin Preset

`wanderlust`

## Biome / Context

Idle Village event notification card (`GoblinEventLabPage`, trailer/lab only).
