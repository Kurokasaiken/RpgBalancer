# Identity — piastrina icona

## Anchor
A small square brass plate, ornate. Inside its rim a recessed well of deep
petrol-black lacquer. On the well sits ONE object — painted oil-impasto (or a
cut-out relief) — centered, frontal, silhouette-filling ~75% of the well.

## Signature (ottone/nero — non-negotiable)
- Brass = the Nav champagne gold of `public/assets/ui/hud-real/nav/bar.webp` —
  hand-cast, warm key light upper-left, teal shadow lower-right.
- Well = deep petrol lacquer (the controls golden's inner ground), not grey,
  never brown.
- Emerald allowed ONLY as small stud accents (same tolerance as the controls
  golden). No purple/magenta anywhere.

## Object law (bibbia §11.2-11.4, unchanged)
- One silhouette, frontal, symmetric in shape; asymmetry lives in wear/light.
- Legible at 11-20px: if it needs detail, it is wrong — simplify.
- No skulls (threat reads as claw marks / crossed lances / broken crown).
- No text, letters, numbers, watermarks. No plastic 3D look, no flat vector.

## Cut-out option ("dipinte/cut-out")
The object may also read as a small die-cut relief pinned on the well — same
materials, a hair of rim-light separating it from the lacquer.
