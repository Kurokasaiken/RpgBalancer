> SDXL con bordo dipinto, R-136). Le tre icone ora nascono dal kit dei prompt d'icona

## Why this asset exists

The `/game` resource plaque (`ResourceReadout`) shows gold, food and wood. The
R-136 painted medallions carried their own bronze rim and teal disc: a painted
frame inside a coded frame, too small to read at 18–22 px. PLAN-058 T1.1 makes
the icon 28–30 px and attaches it to the number, Roster-style; the plaque
already supplies border and lacquer.

## What it teaches

Resources are *things in the world*: a struck coin, a scored loaf, split logs.
They are cast in the same worn bronze with teal lacquer as every painted icon,
with one patina accent each (matte gold, baked amber, honey timber), so the
plaque reads as one material family with the Roster.

## Pillar

Lacquer Atlas HUD material (bibbia §11.3): worn bronze on teal lacquer, light
from the upper left, no shiny gold, no leaves or trinkets, **no painted frame**
— the border is the code's job.

## Contract

- One painted object per resource, no rim, no disc, no background.
- Prompts: `.mw/specs/icons/res-{gold,food,wood}/prompt.md` (GPT image, Gemini,
  Grok), generated by `scripts/gen-icon-prompts.py`, lint `scripts/lint-icon-prompts.py`.
- Master 512 → 128 / 64 / 32 WebP with alpha in `public/assets/ui/resources/`
  (PLAN-058 T3.3). Must read at 24 px and stay distinct from each other in greyscale.
- The R-136 files `public/assets/fantasy_ui/icon_{gold,food,wood}.webp` stay in use
  until the anchors are approved (Gate F2) and mounted (T3.3).
