# Destiny Astrolabe — Asset Identity

**Component ID:** `destiny-astrolabe`
**Type:** UI component / interactive artifact
**Status:** exploration

---

## Silhouette

A circular brass instrument, roughly 1:1 aspect, surrounded by an ornate but slightly asymmetric frame. The center is a dark obsidian disc where the ball travels. Concentric rings and obelisks rise from the center. The overall shape is a medallion, not a window.

## Materials

- **Outer frame:** Baroque Sun-Bronze (weathered, micro-scratches, patina spots), asymmetric finials.
- **Inner field:** polished obsidian with a faint radial gradient and teal caustic reflections.
- **Obelisks (white):** ivory/crystal markers for the player’s skill values.
- **Goo (black):** dark indigo tar-like boundary for the difficulty, with organic blobby edges.
- **Ball:** molten gold/amber marble with a streaking trail.
- **Result seal:** small bronze coin for success, dark crystal for failure.

## Colors

- **Gold family:** #fce890, #e4b048, #c9a227, #8a5a18, #602c08, #2e1a06
- **Teal shadows:** #0a1216, #0a1114, #02020b, rgba(16,110,95,.35)
- **Ivory:** #f0efe4
- **Azure glow:** #3fa9f5
- **Magenta (wound):** #ff3df0
- **Indigo (death):** #5a6bff
- **No grey, no brown, no mud.**

## Kill list (must not contain)

- Text, watermark, signature.
- Perfect symmetry; the frame can be balanced but not mirrored.
- Flat digital surfaces; every metal part must feel tattile.
- Sci-fi elements (wires, screens, holograms).
- Grim/dark blood, skulls, decay.
- Modern UI (buttons, sliders, progress bars) inside the artifact.

## Purpose

Display a D100 skill check resolution with 5 possible outcomes (bigwin, win, almost, fail, epicfail) and secondary wound/death risk.

## Variants needed

- **Empire / gilded-observatory:** heavy bronze, obsidian, prismatic Solar Triumph light.
- **Wilderness / rustic-orrery:** lighter brass, timber, parchment, azure sky reflections.
