# Prompt Gemini — ancore icone (aggiornato 2026-10-14, pane v3)

Un prompt per icona, da incollare così com'è in Gemini. Sfondo magenta #FF00FF, quadrato 1:1.

## poi-goblin-camp

```text
Paint a single game icon, square 1:1.

SUBJECT: A squat hide tent with a dark arched doorway in its front, the sagging canvas tied at the top with a red cord; patina accent: cinnabar on the cord.

STYLE — Wanderlust painted icon, kit v1. A single emblem object for a fantasy strategy game HUD, centred and seen from the front, with a bold symmetrical silhouette that fills about 80% of the square; it must read as one solid shape at 24 pixels and stay distinct in greyscale. MATERIAL: hand-cast worn bronze, rubbed warm and bright on every raised edge and darker in the hollows, with deep teal lacquer pooled in each recess; the only other colour is the single patina accent named in the subject. Matte metal, never polished. LIGHT: one warm white key light from the upper left, cool deep teal shadow on the lower right; a strong value split between the lit face and the shadow side. The shape is symmetrical; the asymmetry lives only in the wear and the light. RENDERING: painted game icon, rich oil impasto in the manner of Jaime Jones on the worn surfaces, a crisp clean silhouette edge, tactile weight, rude beauty; luminous, never black murk; shadows are deep teal, NO grey, NO muddy brown.

STRICT RULES: no text, letters, numbers or runes; no skulls or bones; no frame, border, ring, disc, plaque or badge behind the object; no leaves, vines, flowers or sprigs; no small extra objects, debris or scattered props; no gems, glossy reflections or shiny polished gold; no ground, floor shadow, scene or background texture; no dramatic perspective or three-quarter tilt; no flat vector look, no 3D-render plastic, no cartoon outline; no grim decay, no grey, no mud.

OUTPUT: square 1:1 image, the object alone on a perfectly flat solid magenta background (#FF00FF) with no gradient and no shadow on it; the background will be keyed out and the icon reduced to 512, 128, 64 and 32 px.
```
