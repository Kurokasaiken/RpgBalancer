**Context:** Shift from Idle Village to Archmage: Transcend the Transcendence - Spell-creature lifecycle + Mental Palace management

> [!NOTE]
> This document adapts the previous market research for the Archmage direction. Core monetization principles remain (fair entry pack, whale scaling), but the value proposition and target audience shift significantly.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Archmage targets the **"Strategic Idle RPG"** niche - players who want depth, narrative, and meaningful progression without time-intensive grinding. The spell-creature lifecycle and mental palace mechanics create a unique positioning between **incremental management** and **tactical RPG**.

- **Opportunity:** The "pet/creature collector" + idle hybrid is underserved on Steam. Players who enjoyed *Griftlands*, *Slay the Spire*, and *Darkest Dungeon* but want less intense sessions.
- **Positioning:** **Steam-first premium** with mobile PWA as companion/long-tail. The mental palace theme resonates strongly with PC players who appreciate "second-screen" gameplay.
- **Unique Selling Point (USP):** Semi-sentient spell-creatures with mood systems + infinite mental palace customization. Each run feels unique due to risky reciprocity with demons.
- **Monetization Fit:** **€12.99 premium** with **DLC expansion model**. The creature collector market expects premium pricing (see *Pokemon*, *Stardew Valley*, *Griftlands*).
- **Realistic Year 1 Revenue:** **€35k - €80k** assuming strong Steam launch (5000+ wishlists) and mobile companion adoption.
- **Lifecycle framing:** Premium games with DLC expansions can achieve **Lifetime Revenue ≈ 3–5× Year 1** if content updates maintain quality.

## 3. Pricing Strategy

### 3.1 Base Game: €12.99 (Premium, Single Purchase)

**Justification:**
- **Scope:** 15-25 hours main campaign, 60+ hours completionist
- **Systems:** Creature lifecycle (3 stages), 5 transcendence paths, mental palace customization
- **Comparables:** 
  - *Griftlands*: €19.99 (card-based RPG)
  - *Monster Sanctuary*: €19.99 (creature collector)
  - *Slay the Spire*: €11.99 (roguelike deckbuilder)
  - *Darkest Dungeon*: €24.99 (gothic RPG)

**Steam Strategy:**
- Launch at €12.99, target 20% discount during sales (€10.39)
- Wishlist conversion target: 15-18% (standard for indie RPGs)
- Bundle with future DLC at 25% discount

### 3.2 DLC Expansion Roadmap

#### **DLC 1: The Demonologist's Path** (€6.99)
- New transcendence path focused on demon contracts
- 15+ new spell-creatures with dark themes
- Risky reciprocity mechanics expanded
- New mental palace districts (Abyssal Wing)

#### **DLC 2: The Angelic Choir** (€6.99)
- Light-themed transcendence path
- Holy spell-creatures with healing/support focus
- Cooperative palace mechanics (summon angelic allies)
- New endgame: Celestial Tournament

#### **DLC 3: The Abstract Mind** (€8.99)
- Metaphysical transcendence path
- Reality-bending spell-creatures
- Palace districts that break physics rules
- NG+ mode with true transcendence

### 3.3 Mobile Companion (Free/PWA, Retention Tool)

- **Core Features:** Creature mood monitoring, basic palace management
- **Monetization:** Optional €4.99 "Palace Access" for full mobile editing
- **Sync:** Seamless cloud save between Steam and mobile
- **Purpose:** Retention tool, not primary revenue driver
