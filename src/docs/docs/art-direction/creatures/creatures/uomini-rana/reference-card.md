## Visual Inspiration

- **Creature Design:** Jordu Schell — organic anatomy and creature design
- **Characterization:** Iain McCaig — personality and facial character
- **Painterly language:** Split-Rendering — clean sculptural face/eyes
  (Ruan Jia) vs thick Jaime Jones oil impasto brushstrokes on skin,
  spines, cloth and basalt; Jeff Easley painterly weight
- **Atmosphere (spines):** *The Village* — unsettling, unnatural dorsal
  spines as a specific reference

## Rejected Directions

- **Dinosaur feet / raptor build:** wrong niche — the feet must read
  amphibian or reptilian, narrow and elongated with three digits.
- **Costume-like smooth skin / action figure surface:** kills the
  organic, material texture (wrinkles, pustules, roughness).
- **Anthropomorphic hedgehog:** the spines are decisive for the
  silhouette but must not collapse the design into a quill-man.
- **Rags and tatters:** the garment is a real monastic robe with
  intentional construction — culture, not leftover cloth.
- **Conventional warrior bulk:** two primitive spears must not make it
  massive or read as a standard human warrior.
