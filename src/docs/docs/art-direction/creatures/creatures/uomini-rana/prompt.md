# Uomini-Rana — Image Prompt Injection

```text
A humanoid amphibian-reptilian figure (NOT simply a frog, NOT a toad,
NOT a lizard — unclassifiable) — an alien, primitive, unsettling
people that is unmistakably a culture, not a mere monster.

Silhouette: extremely gangly — legs about 3× the torso, even longer
and stick-thin (torso = shoulders to hips, legs = hips to sole),
long arms, relatively short torso, posture slightly hunched forward.
Shape Language: extreme vertical elongation + irregular spikes.
Anatomy: low wide skull, jaw broad and developed laterally,
small MILKY eyes — clouded, pale, opaque — NOT dominant on the
face; thin low-volume hands with
three clawed digits; narrow elongated three-toed feet,
amphibian/reptilian — never dinosaur feet; frail elongated build
with no imposing musculature.
Skin: intense purple (#7D00B9), rough organic texture rich in
biological detail, marked irregular wrinkles integrated into the
anatomy, amber pustules distributed over the surface — never a
smooth costume or action-figure surface.
Dorsal Spines: large spines emerging from the back, irregular in
distribution, size and orientation, organic like a cross between
porcupine and echidna quills; decisive for the silhouette but the
figure must NOT become an anthropomorphic hedgehog;
The Village unsettling-spine atmosphere.
Clothing: a hooded monastic robe — true intentional monastic
garment construction, coexisting visually with the dorsal spines
and the anomalous proportions; NOT rags, NOT cloth tatters.
Equipment: two primitive spears — simple weapons coherent with a
frail frame, never a massive warrior loadout.
Environment: basalt desert, ancient ruins, austere and mysterious —
suggesting a former or forgotten civilization.
Lighting: austere desert light, deep basalt shadows, prismatic
dust — coherent with Split-Rendering Wanderlust.
Style: Split-Rendering Wanderlust — the small milky amphibian
face/eyes clean and sculptural (Ruan Jia, no visible brushstrokes),
while
skin, wrinkles, pustules, dorsal spines, monastic cloth and basalt
are rendered with THICK Jaime Jones oil impasto: large visible
brushstrokes, heavy paint matter, scratches and organic texture.
Jordu Schell organic anatomy, Iain McCaig facial characterization,
Jeff Easley painterly weight.
Mood: alien, primitive, unsettling — but cultured; a people with
rituals among the ruins, not an animal in clothes.
NO LIST: dinosaur feet, raptor build, costume-like smooth skin,
action-figure surfaces, anthropomorphic hedgehog, rags or tatters,
large dominant eyes, lively glossy eyes, conventional human face,
massive human-warrior
build, more than three digits on hands or feet, sci-fi tech,
flat digital surfaces, thin glazes, airbrushed gradients,
smooth digital paint.
```
