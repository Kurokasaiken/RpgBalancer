# Uomini-Rana — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

TBD — this is a concept card for a sapient race, not yet a declared
inhabitant of Wanderlust: Triumph. No mechanical role has been defined.

## Player Lesson

TBD.

## Desired Emotion

Unsettling, alien, primitive — but never "just a monster". The monastic
garb and primitive spears must communicate *culture*, possibly ritual
practice: the player should wonder who they are and what the ruins mean,
not only whether they are dangerous.

## Desired Player Behavior

TBD.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The world needs sapient peoples beyond the human frontier: this race
occupies the "ancient or forgotten civilization" space, giving the basalt
desert ruins inhabitants that feel native to them.

## Biome

Basalt desert with ancient ruins — austere, mysterious, suggesting a
former or forgotten civilization.

## Family

Unassigned — none of the current families (Forest Parasites, Ancient
Constructs, Mythic Beasts, Beasts) admit humanoid anatomy or sapient
culture. Open decision: create a new family for sapient peoples, or keep
the race outside the family system.
