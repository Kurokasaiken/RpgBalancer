# Uomini-Rana

Working name (Italian for "frog-men"), assigned by the Director on
2026-10-12. **Concept card only** — this entry does NOT declare the race
canonical in Wanderlust: Triumph. Reconstructed from the characteristics
defined in the discussion of 22 August, plus the newer proportion spec.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Creature Type** | Sapient Race (humanoid amphibian/reptilian) |
| **Family** | Unassigned — none of the four existing families admit humanoid anatomy; needs a family decision (possibly a new sapient-peoples family) |
| **Archetype** | Uomini-Rana |
| **Identity Sentence** | A gangly amphibian-reptilian people in monastic robes, bearing primitive spears among basalt ruins — unsettling and primitive, yet unmistakably a culture. |
| **Familiar + Impossible** | Frog/toad humanoid + monastic order + porcupine–echidna dorsal spines |
| **Silhouette Hook** | Legs ~3× torso length, stick-thin, long arms, hunched-forward posture, large irregular dorsal spines |
| **Shape Language** | Extreme vertical elongation + irregular spikes |
| **Creature Verb** | strides, crouches, peers, stands vigil |
| **Ecological / Cultural Niche** | Monastic or ritual culture bound to a basalt desert and ancient ruins — a people, not a beast |

## Why Exists

Introduce a sapient non-human culture that reads as alien and unsettling
without being simply monstrous — candidate inhabitant of the
ancient-civilization layer of the world.

See [design-intent.md](design-intent.md).

## Defining Traits (do not lose during iterations)

1. **Extreme proportions** — legs ~3× torso, stick-thin, long arms,
   relatively short torso, hunched posture.
2. **Apparent fragility** — thin limbs, thin three-clawed hands, narrow
   three-toed feet.
3. **Ambiguous physiognomy** — amphibian face between frog and toad, low
   wide skull, small milky eyes.
4. **Distinctive biology** — wrinkled purple skin (`#7D00B9`), amber
   pustules, large irregular dorsal spines.
5. **Cultural identity** — authentic monastic hood/robe, primitive spears,
   association with basalt ruins.
