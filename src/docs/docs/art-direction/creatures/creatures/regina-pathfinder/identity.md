# Il Pathfinder della Regina — Identity Card

> «A frail servant whose congenital weakness was sculpted into
> precision. He walks the world first, so the court knows where it
> can grow.»

## Archetype

**The Remade Instrument.** A male humanoid servant of the Regina
degli Ibridi — explorer, pathfinder and silent predator, NOT a
warrior. His body was a frail, congenitally anomalous frame that
the queen did not heal but *rebuilt into an instrument*: the
weakness is still legible, sharpened into purpose.

## Affinity Read (family law)

He carries a **total Nm graft across the entire torso** — the
largest surface investment documented so far. By the family rule
(*affinity reads by graft quantity*) he had **low affinity**: the
queen remade almost everything. Contrast with the queen herself,
whose organic body remains dominant.

## Movement

Silent, economical, precise. He reads the ground through his feet
more than the air through his eyes. Mantis-like resting angles;
weight and posture of a predator that observes before it kills.

## Story Trace

The congenital remnant — the receded jaw, the asymmetric skull,
the frail frame — is the weakness the queen refused to erase.
It is what makes him read as *remade*, not *born*.
