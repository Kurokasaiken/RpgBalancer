# Il Pathfinder della Regina — Decision Log

## v0.1.0

**Decision:** creature card + prompt created in the Creature IP
Development System with working name "regina-pathfinder" (R-237,
2026-10-20). Status `draft` — nothing canonical yet.

**Source:** Director-supplied prompt draft («THE QUEEN'S
PATHFINDER», 2026-10-20), corrected against the Nm Mutants family
law and Prompt Engineering V2 before filing.

**Corrections applied to the Director draft:**

- **Tonal register:** «dark fantasy character design», «muted
  atmospheric contrast» → Solar Triumph/Wanderlust register
  (clean directional light, saturated teal shadows, awe not grime).
- **Gold usage:** «fine champagne-gold filaments», «champagne-gold
  filigree near knees/ankles» → rewritten as **thin seam lines at
  graft boundaries** (family law: gold is a seam, never ornament —
  the filigree wording is the exact drift that produced the
  Elesh-Norn risk on the queen).
- **Torso:** «no ordinary human skin across chest/back» kept but
  reframed as a **total Nm graft** — makes him the first
  low-affinity specimen (graft quantity ↔ affinity law, applied).
- **Engravings:** ruby-red incisions on ivory retained —
  **first applied instance of the ruby Neoranga-groove variant**
  (family-level gold-vs-ruby decision still open; this is evidence
  for ruby on non-sovereign members).
- **Eyes:** kept the grown-in irregular compound facets; faint
  «oil-slick» read capped at smoked-nacre surface response so
  prismatic stays confined to veils (family law).
- **Format:** recast into V2 §10.3 fields (IDENTITY SENTENCE,
  FAMILY, SIGNATURE HOOK, CONTRADICTION, MAGIC VECTOR, THE WORLD
  REACTS, SILHOUETTE, MOVEMENT, MATERIALS, COLORS, ANATOMY,
  ENVIRONMENT, LIGHTING, STYLE, MOOD, STORY TRACE, NO LIST).
  Rear-view deliverable moved to generation notes (sheet format).

**Specs kept verbatim from the draft:** lean androgynous frail
frame; elongated forearms with hands near knees; mantis-like
elbow rest; recessed jaw + exposed dry jawbone; irregular worn
human teeth; oversized faceted eyes; full-torso Nm graft;
ruby engravings; functional quiet legs; sensory-precise hands.

- **Engraving semantics** — do marks encode rank/function? TBD.

**Addendum (2026-10-21):** the family gained a **Face Law**
(`family-dna.md` v0.3.0): remade faces must carry accentuated
congenital problems — weak sickly constitution, a visible external
tumor, or an extreme settled wound (Shigurui register: no chin,
mouth opened by cut cheeks, missing nose). This creature is the
**first applied instance** (recessed jaw + exposed jawbone + worn
teeth). The queen is the ruled exception — her wound lives in the
burned left hand.

«Scheda Concept del Pathfinder Osseo» filed in
`reference-card.md`. It declares the host as **GOBLIN** («HEAD &
FACE — GOBLIN + VICISSITUDINE + NM») — `host_family` updated to
`goblin` (existing registry creature). Sheet header says «ex-human
predator» — resolved as goblin host; height 160–165 cm noted.
