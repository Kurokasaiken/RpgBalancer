# Il Pathfinder della Regina — Image Prompt (Prompt Engineering V2, §10.3)

Corrected from a Director-supplied draft (2026-10-20). Corrections
applied vs the draft: tonal register moved from «dark fantasy /
muted atmospheric contrast» to Solar Triumph/Wanderlust; decorative
«champagne-gold filigree» rewritten as seam lines (family Material
Law — gold is never ornament); full-torso Nm surface reframed as a
total graft = low-affinity read; ruby engravings kept as the first
applied instance of the Neoranga ruby-groove variant; recast into
V2 §10.3 fields.

```text
SUBJECT TYPE: Creature / magical entity — Prompt Engineering V2.
IDENTITY SENTENCE: A frail, long-limbed male pathfinder remade by a
vampire queen — a silent scout whose congenital weakness was
sculpted into precision. (NOT a warrior, NOT a handsome hero, NOT

CONTRADICTION: He looks underdeveloped, breakable, unfinished —

FUNCTION & ECOLOGY: The queen's pathfinder — explorer, forward

MAGIC VECTOR: The same material law as the queen, in a rougher,
```

## Notes for generation

- **Composition:** full-body three-quarter view, neutral unsettling
  stance (shoulders drawn in, hands low, fingers slightly curled);
  face clearly readable (eyes, recessed chin, exposed jaw). Include
  a smaller coherent **rear three-quarter view** of the same figure
  to show the Nm structure and engravings across the back — the back
  is designed as deliberately as the front.
- **Style reference:** the approved queen references
  (`public/assets/creatures/reference/regina-ibrida*.jpg|png`) are
  the primary material/lighting anchor — same Nm material language,
  rougher execution; do NOT copy her silhouette.
- **Engraving law:** ruby-red grooves are *cut into* the ivory and
  pigment-filled — saturated dark red, never neon, never glowing,
  never painted on skin. First applied case of the ruby variant
  (gold vs ruby still TBD at family level — this creature is
  evidence for ruby on non-sovereign members).
- **Eye watchpoint:** facets must look grown-in and wet-organic;
  if they drift toward goggles/insect domes, regenerate — that is
  the single most common failure for this design.
- The faint iridescence on the eyes is a *surface response* of the
  faceted tissue (smoked nacre), not a prismatic veil — keep it
  faint; prismatic stays confined to veils/fragments per family law.
