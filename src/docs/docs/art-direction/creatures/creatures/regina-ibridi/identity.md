# Regina degli Ibridi

Working name (Italian for "Queen of the Hybrids"), from the Director's
brief of 2026-10-20: *«tipo il leader di una fazione di ibridi»*.
**Concept card only** — not yet canonical in Wanderlust: Triumph.
Design input: external ChatGPT design discussion pasted by the Director
(R-237) + same-day Director rulings on role and materials.

## Why Exists

Give the aberration a face and a voice. She is the faction leader the
player can meet: recurring presence, quest/world actor, embodiment of
what the Ibridi offer and threaten. Anchors the aberration to world
memory — the player recognizes *who she is*, not just *what she is*
(cf. NARRATIVE.md M-03/M-04).

See [design-intent.md](design-intent.md).

## Defining Traits (do not lose during iterations)

1. **The shield, not a crown** — flattened Cephalaspis shield sweeping backward from the crown, never protruding laterally,
   continuous ivory above, opened arches behind, prismatic light beneath.
2. **Ruby, not flesh** — muscle reads as translucent gemstone; ivory
   reads as carved nobility; joints are metal.
3. **Attire as anatomy** — heels, train and shawl evoke evening dress
   but are body and mutation, never worn.
4. **Two clocks** — Vicissitudine mass moves heavy and close; Nm
   ornament floats light and delayed.
5. **The person persists** — face, posture and grace keep her a *she*,
   a leader with presence, not a spectacle.
6. **The burned left hand** — vampire + mutation could not or would not
   heal it: the one scar that tells there was a before.
