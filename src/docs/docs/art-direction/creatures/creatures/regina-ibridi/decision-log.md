# Regina degli Ibridi — Decision Log

## v0.1.0

**Decision:** Concept card created in the Creature IP Development
System with working name "regina-ibridi" (R-237, 2026-10-20). New
family **Nm Mutants** filed alongside (`families/nm-mutants/`), status
`draft` — nothing here is canonical yet.

**Source:** external ChatGPT design discussion pasted verbatim by the
Director + same-day Director rulings.

**Specs locked in this version:**

- **Role:** leader of the Ibridi — a faction of transformed beings
  («tipo il leader di una fazione di ibridi»).
- **Family model:** C — family of process; members declare
  `host_family` + `aberration_stage`; more than one Nm-mutant lineage
  allowed (Nm-dominant vs Vicissitudine-dominant).
- **Elegant female silhouette**: evening-dress figure kept legible —
  anatomy evokes attire, never costume.
- **Head:** recognizably human face + Cephalaspis-inspired cranial
  shield (anatomy, not helmet); rear opens into bone
  arches/ribs/filaments.
- **Materials (Director rulings 2026-10-20):**
  - muscle/flesh → **ruby-like** translucent mass, not raw flesh;
  - bone → **noble ivory**;
  - joints and small details → **metallic**;
  - beneath/inside opened bone (esp. the cephalic shield) →
    **prismatic / nacre-like** layer.
- **Strascico** = Vicissitudine-dominant: bone/muscle/tendon mass at
  rest, gathering under the silhouette.
- **Scialle** = Nm-dominant: ethereal jellyfish float — metal filigree
  ribs, fragmented crystal membranes, hanging filaments, never
  touching skin.
- **Nm ornament law:** floating, autonomous, uses empty space — no
  embedded jewelry; khalkhal/barefoot-sandal composition kept only as
  suspended-segment ornament.
- **Anti-references:** Elesh Norn formula (blade crown + white armor +
  red flesh), industrial biomechanics, gore, tentacles as main element,
  clothing logic.

**Addendum (2026-10-20):**

- Family renamed `aberrants` → **`nm-mutants`** — «aberranti è troppo
  generico, parliamo di mutazioni di Nm/sci-fi» (Director).
- **She is a vampire**; her **left hand is burned** — Director spec.
  Registered as the card's story trace: the one wound the mutation
  refused to heal. Citation added: *Vampire: The Masquerade* —
  Vicissitude (Tzimisce flesh-craft) is the namesake of the organic
  grammar.

## v0.2.0

**Decision:** The mutation is a **kit grafted onto the unchanged
host** — the host keeps fur, feathers, colors; marks are never painted
on the body (Director, 2026-10-20). Material Law of three roles:
**ivory = structure** (every reconstructed piece, whatever the copied
shape), **champagne gold = seam** (thin vein at each tissue↔graft
boundary — never ornament), **prismatic = light** (gasoline-iridescent
veils/membranes only). **Affinity reads by graft quantity** (more
grafts = less affinity — established rule). **Neoranga-style geometric
marks** engraved on ivory, grooves gold or ruby-red — candidate, TBD.
Her left hand now carries both processes: ivory/gold/prism over living
irregular burned tissue.

**Reason:** makes the kit applicable to *any* creature — solves the
animal-members problem (no painting on fur) and makes chimerism
legible by material, not by anatomy.

**Consequence:** `family-dna.md` restructured (Two Processes, Material
Law, Legibility Rules, forbidden: ornamental gold / diffuse prismatic /
painted markings); `prompt.md` MATERIALS/MAGIC VECTOR/ANATOMY/NO LIST
realigned; rejected-directions gained «Gold as Ornament» and «Painted
Markings on the Host».

**Open decisions:**

- **Canonical name** — «Regina degli Ibridi» is a working name.
- **Cosmology** — what Vicissitudine and Nm *are* in-world (forces,
  entities, contagion?): `world`-authority, needs Director ruling.
- **Pillar tag** — `third-pole` is a candidate; needs ratification.
- **Court/biome, world tier, animation grammar, player behavior** —
  all TBD.
- **Faction canonization** — the Ibridi as a registered faction of the
  world (narrative doc, not just a creature card).
- **Prompt** — `prompt.md` written 2026-10-20 on template V2 §10.3
  (creature/magical entity; human template rejected — the defining
  fields are MAGIC VECTOR/FAMILY/WORLD REACTS). First generated images
  pending; suggested scope: head + shoulders for the shield contract.
