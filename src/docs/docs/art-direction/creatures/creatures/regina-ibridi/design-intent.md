# Regina degli Ibridi — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Major NPC: the leader of the **Ibridi**, the faction of beings
mutated by the Nm (Vicissitudine × Nm ornament). She is a **vampire** —
undead sovereignty made visible — with her **left hand burned**, the
scar the mutation refused to heal. She is the faction's face: a
recurring presence the player can meet, recognize and negotiate with. Candidate roles: envoy of the third pole, source
of Nm-mutant quests/knowledge, living measure of what the mutation
offers and costs. Mechanical role TBD.

## Player Lesson

The Nm mutation is not a disease that destroys: it re-sculpts into a
different order of beauty — and it has a voice. Meeting her teaches
the player that the transformed are a *people* with a sovereign, not
a plague.

## Desired Emotion

Reverent unease. The player should feel awe and slight intimidation
tempered by recognition — she is unmistakably a *person*, a sovereign
receiving an audience. Never disgust, never plain horror.

## Desired Player Behavior

TBD — candidates: approach with caution, seek her court for
Nm-mutant knowledge/boons, read her faction's mood through the state
of her floating architecture.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The world's two pillars (Wilderness/Rude Beauty, Empire/Solar Triumph)
have no face for the third pole. The Ibridi give the Nm mutation a
society and her design literally fuses the two pillar grammars:
Vicissitudine reads Wilderness, Nm reads Empire. She is the collision
made sovereign.
