# Regina degli Ibridi — Image Prompt (Prompt Engineering V2, §10.3)

Template: creature / magical entity. Chosen over the human template
(§10.2) because the Motore di Unicità fields that define her — MAGIC
VECTOR, FAMILY, THE WORLD REACTS — exist only in §10.3; her human face
is preserved as the clean split-rendering focal point (cf. Cervo-Faro
glass antlers, §10.5).

```text
SUBJECT TYPE: Creature / magical entity — Prompt Engineering V2.
IDENTITY SENTENCE: A vampire queen whose evening attire is living
anatomy — an ivory cranial shield, bony heels, a train of bone and
sinew, and a shawl of floating metal and crystal. (NOT a biomechanical
queen, NOT an armored machine-priestess.)
FAMILY: Nm Mutants — a family of process: the Nm graft a kit onto the
unchanged host — ivory for structure, champagne gold for seams,
prismatic light for veils; Vicissitudine sculpts the living tissue.
SIGNATURE HOOK: A flattened ivory cranial shield sweeping backward
from the crown like the head of a prehistoric armored fish — it
extends behind her head, never protruding laterally — beneath its
opened arches and ribs, a prismatic mother-of-pearl light.
CONTRADICTION: The most composed sovereign presence in the world —
yet her left hand is burned: the one wound her transformation never
healed, kept uncovered like a vow.
FUNCTION & ECOLOGY: Sovereign of the Ibridi, the faction of the
transformed; she receives, judges and remakes — her own body is the
proof of what her court offers.
MAGIC VECTOR: Mutation obeys matter — every reconstructed piece is
carved noble ivory (the color says the Nm made it, the shape says what
it copied); a single thin champagne-gold vein marks each seam between
living tissue and graft; gasoline-iridescent prismatic light lives
only inside transparent veils and membranes; on the organic side,
flesh becomes translucent ruby and sinew stays dark and irregular.
THE WORLD REACTS: Small metal fragments drift and orbit around her
shoulders; the air beneath the cranial shield glimmers with iridescent
light; her train slowly unfurls when she manifests.
SILHOUETTE: Three-tier — flattened ivory shield sweeping backward
behind the head, never sideways (top), slender feminine figure poised
on anatomical heels (middle), a heavy low train at the base and a
light floating shawl as halo. Unmistakable at any size.
SHAPE LANGUAGE: Vertical feminine figure × backward-swept shield +
floating arcs.
MOVEMENT: Glides, poses, unfurls, drifts — the train moves heavy and
close to the ground; the shawl floats with liquid, jellyfish-like
delay.
MATERIALS: Vampire pale skin and ruby-translucent flesh, raw sculpted
bone (Vicissitudine side) + the Nm kit: carved ivory grafts, thin
champagne-gold seam lines at every graft boundary, transparent
gasoline-iridescent veils and membranes for all floating parts.
Geometric ritual marks engraved into the ivory, grooves filled with
the same champagne gold.
COLORS: Ivory, deep ruby red, pale skin, champagne-gold seam lines,
prismatic iridescence confined to veils; deep teal shadows. NO grey,
NO brown, NO mud.
ANATOMY: Adult female humanoid base — a beautiful, calm, recognizably
human face; no hair; the cranial shield is grown anatomy, not a
helmet and sweeps backward from the crown, never sideways; heels are
ivory grafts, not shoes; muscle reads as gemstone, never as wound;
the burned left hand carries both processes: ivory
and gold grafts over living irregular burned tissue. NO clothes, NO
worn jewelry, NO tentacle mass, NO painted markings on skin.
ENVIRONMENT: Her court — a ceremonial interior being rewritten by the
Nm: suspended filigree arcs, veils of crystal, dark polished floor
catching her reflection.
LIGHTING: Solar Triumph — blinding white light from above, deep cool
teal shadows, prismatic dust motes answering her underlayer.
STYLE: Split-rendering — the human face clean and sculptural (Ruan
Jia, luminous subsurface, zero brushstrokes); body, train, ornaments
and court in thick Jaime Jones oil impasto, broad visible strokes;
crystal membranes and filigree edges kept crisp like blown glass.
Jeff Easley painterly weight.
MOOD: Reverent awe + unsettling beauty.
STORY TRACE: The burned left hand — a scar from the night she changed;
she never covers it.
NO LIST: blade crown, white organic armor combined with red exposed
flesh (Elesh Norn formula), helmet, hair, clothes or costume, jewelry
embedded in flesh, gold filigree or arabesques (gold is a thin seam,
not ornament), diffuse prismatic sheen on solid surfaces, painted
tattoos or markings on skin, gore or raw muscle, tentacles as the
main element, sci-fi tubes/gears/implants, generic D&D vampire,
symmetry, T-pose, standing portrait, grim decay, mud, brown.
```

## Notes for generation

- **First round suggestion:** scope to head + shoulders to validate the
  cephalic shield / prismatic underlayer / clean-face contract before
  attempting the full silhouette (train + shawl + heels compete for
  attention and are easier to tune once the head is signed off).
- The burned left hand must read as a *scar*, not an injury in
  progress — living irregular tissue carrying ivory/gold grafts; if a
  generation renders it as open wound/gore it violates the family
  material law.
- Engraved marks on the ivory use **champagne-gold-filled grooves**
  in this prompt; the **ruby-red groove variant** is a pending
  Director decision (family-dna, Neoranga marks — both options open).
- Court environment is a placeholder (TBD in `design-intent.md`): for
  the head study a dark neutral ground is preferable.
