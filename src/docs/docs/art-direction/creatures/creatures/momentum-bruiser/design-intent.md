# Momentum Bruiser — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Provide an early empire brute that teaches positioning and crowd control.

## Player Lesson

Momentum can be redirected, never stopped head-on.

## Desired Emotion

Adrenaline and respect.

## Desired Player Behavior

Dodge, lure into obstacles, avoid direct clash.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The Empire needs a force-of-nature antagonist that is not clever, only overwhelming.

## Biome

Imperial arena or basalt canyon.

## World Tier

Early/Mid — Stage II — Common.
