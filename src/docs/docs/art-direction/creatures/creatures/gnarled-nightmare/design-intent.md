# Gnarled Nightmare — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Teach the player that forests must be prepared before entering.

## Player Lesson

Observation before confrontation.

## Desired Emotion

Curiosity mixed with caution.

## Desired Player Behavior

Use preparation items and observe the environment before entering unknown forests.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The world should reward knowledge and preparation, not only combat power.

## Biome

Ancient sun-drenched forest at the wilderness edge.

## World Tier

Early/Mid — Stage II — Rare.
