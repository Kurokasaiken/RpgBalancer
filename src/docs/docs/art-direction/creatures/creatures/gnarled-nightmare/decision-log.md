# Gnarled Nightmare — Decision Log

## v0.1.0

**Decision:** Creature initially designed with a humanoid face.

**Rejected because:**

- The player interpreted it as a goblin.
- Violated Family DNA non-humanoid face rule.

**Impact:** Changed anatomy rule for entire family; reinforced "no humanoid faces" in Forest Parasites.

## v0.2.0

**Decision:** Converted into parasitic wooden organism.

**Reason:**

- Better aligned with Wanderlust:
  - wonder instead of horror
  - ecology instead of evil
  - environmental storytelling

## v0.3.0

**Decision:** Moved from standalone creature to founding member of Forest Parasites family.

**Reason:** Shared DNA with Rootling and Ancient Root King became evident.

## v1.0.0

**Canonical decision:** Creature becomes canonical reference for Forest Parasites family.

**Approved by:** art_director, narrative_director.
