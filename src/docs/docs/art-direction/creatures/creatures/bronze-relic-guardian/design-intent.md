# Bronze Relic Guardian — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Introduce the player to empire-side threats that cannot be rushed.

## Player Lesson

Patience, timing, and respect for ancient power.

## Desired Emotion

Awe and dread of interruption.

## Desired Player Behavior

Observe wake/sleep cycles, avoid frontal assault, exploit ceremonial interruptions.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The Empire needs a face of ancient weight that is not pure villainy.

## Biome

Basalt desert under indigo prismatic sky.

## World Tier

Mid — Stage II — Rare.
