# Gohul Combattente — Identity Card

> «THE HYBRID SERVANT / LOW-RANK SERVANT / AFFLICTED» — title
> printed on the Director's reference image.

## Archetype

**The Afflicted Thrall.** Combat variant of the gohul caste —
a base humanoid broken into a screaming weapon. Where the explorer
was remade into sight, this one was remade into violence — and the
face tells you it was not a gift.
