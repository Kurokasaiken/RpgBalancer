# Goblin — Image Prompt Injection

## Blocco per soggetto singolo / primo piano (ritratti, hero art)

```text
A goblin — small wiry raider, knee-to-waist of a man —
matte green skin, huge pointed ears, long nose, narrow
sharp-featured face, lean corded muscle, hunched springy
posture like a drawn wire.

Gear: crude handmade craft — hide leather straps, hemp
cord, a wooden club or stone-tipped wooden spear;
ochre and crimson war-paint stripes on skin and gear.

Character: ferocious swarm — his energy is the mob,
shrieking confidence only in numbers.

Style: thick oil impasto (Jaime Jones matter on hide,
wood and green skin); ears and war-paint catch clean
light where it strikes.

NO LIST: orc bulk, troll scale, pig snout, metal armor,
cute/comic expressions, glow eyes, gore, heroic poses,
green-skinned human proportions.
```

## Blocco per massa/sciame a distanza (scene ambientali)

Usare questo blocco quando i goblin sono elemento di scena
(combattimento, caccia, agguato) — la regola delle quest è
massa/sagome: questo descriptor rende la massa *riconoscibile*
senza disegnare individui.

```text
a swarm of small wiry goblin raiders — knee-to-waist
height, huge pointed ears readable in silhouette,
flashes of ochre-and-crimson war-paint, crude wooden
clubs and stone-tipped spears raised over the mass —
a screaming tide of green shapes, mass and motion only,
no faces, no individual anatomy
```

## Negative / NO LIST (completa)

```
orc, troll, ogre, pig snout, metal armor, steel weapons,
cute, comic, cartoon, glow eyes, gore, heroic single goblin,
human-scale green skin, fantasy cliché prosthetics, clean
smooth skin, sci-fi, text
```
