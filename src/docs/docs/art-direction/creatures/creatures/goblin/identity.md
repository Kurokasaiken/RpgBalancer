# Goblin — Identity Card

## Authority

- Family: — (unassigned; swarm raiders of the wild valleys)
- Status: `candidate` — identità di lavoro validata dal Director,
  non ancora canon world-truth
- Source of truth: this file
- Last certified: 2026-10-13
- Nota di contesto: i goblin sono **archetipi narrativi temporanei**
  (R-172) — compaiono come antagonisti di quest/raid, non come popolo
  con lore canonico proprio. La scheda esiste per **coerenza visiva**
  delle scene, non per fondare una specie.

## Silhouette

- Small wiry raiders — knee-to-waist of a man, lean corded muscle,
  fast nervous movement, hunched springy posture.
- Huge pointed ears (the read at any distance), long nose,
  sharp-featured narrow faces.
- Never alone by design: they exist as a **screaming swarm** —
  a mass of small shapes, numbers as their weapon.

## Materials & gear

- Crude craft, handmade: hide leather, hemp cord, wooden clubs,
  stone-tipped wooden spears, wicker-and-hide shields.
- **Ochre and crimson war-paint** stripes on skin and gear —
  the visual link to the goblin war totem (V002-hero asset).

## Palette

- Skin: classic green — deep, earthy, matte (not neon, not glossy).
- Gear: hide browns rendered materico (rude beauty, never grime),
  hemp beige, war-paint ochre/cinnabar.
- Palette di scena: pigmenti Wanderlust (ultramarine, Veronese, cinabro,
  ambra; ombre colorate mai grigio/marrone/fango). La luce è libera per
  scena; il verde goblin è l'unico colore identity-locked.

## Kill list (no)

- No orc bulk, no troll scale, no pig snout.
- No metal armor, no heroic lone goblin, no cute/comic expressions.
- No readable individual character inside scene masses
  (quest rule: mass/silhouette only).
- No gore, no glow-eyes cliché.
