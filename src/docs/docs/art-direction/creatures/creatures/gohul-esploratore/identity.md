# Gohul Esploratore — Identity Card

> «Low-rank servant: a base creature hollowed into a gohul — the
> court's expendable eyes.»

## Archetype

**The Expendable Scout.** A **gohul** — Director's spelling of the
vampire-lore *ghoul* class: low-rank servants made by transforming
base humanoids (or non-humanoids) into thralls (Director ruling,
2026-10-21). The explorer variant reads the world at ground level
for the court.

## Silhouette & Proportions (from Director image)

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Frame** | Gaunt, sinewy, crouched — permanently poised low to the ground, elongated forelimbs able to touch the floor |
| **Head/Face** | Moth-like read: filament antennae, layered proboscis-mouth flaps instead of a jaw; **asymmetric eyes** — one greatly oversized faceted prismatic eye, one smaller pale eye; hair-like filaments (Face Law: the face is consumed into insectoid unreadability) |
| **Eyes** | Compound facets iridescent blue-violet — grown in, clustered |
| **Skin grafts** | Clusters of small iridescent compound eyes/pits along shoulder, chest, thigh and forearm; small eye-rows down one arm — *sensory* grafts: the body is covered in sight |
| **Skin** | Pale grey-beige, lean muscle, scattered wounds reading as seams |

## Material & Color

- Pale grey-beige host skin, dark sinew, bruised tones.
- Iridescent blue-violet compound facets (sensory grafts).
- Deep teal environment shadows.

## Deviations flagged vs family law

- **Prismatic on skin** — RESOLVED by ruling (2026-10-21): on
  low-affinity hosts the iridescent surfaces are **gouged prismatic
  wounds** carved into living tissue — a feature of harsh grafting,
  not the veil rule. The eye-cluster pits read exactly as this.
- **Insectoid/ichthyic face** vs Face Law — reframed by ruling
  (2026-10-21): the Nm **upgraded a too-weak host with donor DNA** —
  ichthyic traits (compound eye-clusters, sucker-pits). Not origin
  fauna: deliberate borrowed improvement. Host was a living
  non-vampire creature.

## Kill List

- NO clean handsome face (servant caste)

## Story Trace

The eye grafts: the court remade him into a thing that *sees* —
his body is an instrument of observation, his own face spent first.
