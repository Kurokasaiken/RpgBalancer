# Emerald Scale Serpent — Design Intent

## Gameplay Purpose

Reward the player with wonder and a sense of smallness before empire ruins.

## Player Lesson

Some creatures are not enemies; they are monuments.

## Desired Emotion

Awe and reverence.

## Desired Player Behavior

Observe, avoid startling, seek hidden paths around it.

## Why Wanderlust Needs This

The world needs ancient grandeur that is not hostile, only indifferent.

## Biome

Tropical jungle ruins with marble columns.

## World Tier

Mid/Late — Stage II — Epic.
