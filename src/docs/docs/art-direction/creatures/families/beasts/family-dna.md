# Beasts — Family DNA

## World Pillar

Empire — basalt deserts, colossal architecture, imperial arenas.

## Core Fantasy

Monstrous predators and brutes shaped by empire, trial, and raw force.

## Mandatory Traits

- Heavy animal or humanoid-animal hybrid body
- Asymmetric silhouette
- Raw, brutal materials
- Predatory or aggressive movement
- Arena/trial cultural context

## Forbidden

- Clean human faces
- Sci-fi tech
- Perfect symmetry
- Noble/chivalric armor
- Cartoonish musculature

## Emotional Space

- Fear
- Respect
- Adrenaline

## Evolution Grammar

- **Stage I:** young beast or scavenger
- **Stage II:** arena predator or brute enforcer
- **Stage III:** legendary tyrant or champion

## Material Palette

- **Primary:** obsidian, raw sienna, violet, heavy bronze
- **Secondary:** basalt, ritual scars, cracked leather
- **Forbidden:** chrome, plastic, rubber, clean steel

## Visual Inspiration

- **Creature Design:** Jeff Easley monsters, 80s heroic fantasy antagonists
- **Material:** cracked stone, rough bronze, volcanic glass
- **Movement:** charging, heavy stomp, sudden lunge
