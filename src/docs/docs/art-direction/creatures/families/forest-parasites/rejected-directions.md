# Forest Parasites — Rejected Directions

These directions were explored and rejected for the entire family.

## Goblin Archetype

**Rejected because:**

- too humanoid
- generic fantasy marker
- violates family "non-humanoid face" rule

## Ent Archetype

**Rejected because:**

- too noble and static
- lacks predatory threat
- does not fit "parasite" fantasy

## Horror Monster

**Rejected because:**

- violates Wanderlust tone of wonder, not disgust
- shifts emotional space from awe to revulsion
