# Forest Parasites — Family DNA

## World Pillar

Wilderness — ancient, sun-drenched forests at the edge of the known world.

## Core Fantasy

Ancient forest folklore corrupted into living organisms.

## Mandatory Traits

- Organic material (living wood, resin, black sap)
- Asymmetrical silhouette
- Environmental traces before the creature appears
- Slow unnatural movement, then sudden bursts
- Small warm glowing eyes or no eyes at all

## Forbidden

- Armor
- Weapons
- Humanoid faces
- Technology / sci-fi elements
- Perfect symmetry
- Human hands or humanoid skull proportions

## Emotional Space

- Curiosity
- Unease
- Punctual awe

## Evolution Grammar

- **Stage I:** small parasite, hidden, harmless alone
- **Stage II:** territorial predator, ambush
- **Stage III:** ecosystem-level catastrophe

## Material Palette

- **Primary:** living wood, obsidian, black sap
- **Secondary:** ritual silk, amber, bone
- **Forbidden:** metal armor, leather, plastic, chrome

## Visual Inspiration

- **Creature Design:** Hayao Miyazaki forest spirits, European folklore wood spirits
- **Material:** antique bronze sculpture, ancient tree roots
- **Movement:** slow puppet-like animation, predator freezing behavior
