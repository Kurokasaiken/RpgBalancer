# Nm Mutants — Rejected Directions

These directions were explored and rejected for the entire family.

## Elesh Norn Clone

**Rejected because:**

- the reference was named by the Director as territory to *avoid*, not
  to copy (R-237 discussion)
- blade-crown + white biomechanical armor + red exposed flesh already
  reads «biomechanical queen» — our identity must read *ancient and
  mutable*, not *perfect machine-theology*
- armor logic contradicts the family law: nothing here is worn

## Industrial Biomechanics

**Rejected because:**

- tubes, cables, gears and implants shift the family into sci-fi
  cybernetics, outside Wanderlust fantasy
- the reference image used for the head (tubed/mechanical rear skull)
  was retained only for its *structural complexity* — the materials
  must stay bone/sinew/metal-filigree/crystal
- Giger is inspiration for organic-mechanical *fusion*, not for
  industrial plumbing

## Horror Gore

**Rejected because:**

- open wounds, raw red muscle and viscera shift emotion from awe to
  revulsion — violates the Wanderlust tone (wonder, not disgust)
- Director ruling: muscle reads as ruby-like translucent mass; bone is
  noble ivory. Anatomy is sculpted, not injured
- consistent with Prismatic Wanderlust «no grim/mud»

## Amorphous Tentacle Mass

**Rejected because:**

- tentacles as the main element lose the silhouette — the family is
  *structured* transformation, not goo
- the Director explicitly preferred the elegant-silhouette direction
  over tentacles as primary feature
- resonates with desiderata v9: adversary geometry is allowed to be
  structured, not necessarily amorphous

## Applied Jewelry / Costume

**Rejected because:**

- gems set into flesh, chains fastened to skin, or any clothing logic
  turn mutation into fashion — «un po' banale» (Director)
- the ornament must be *autonomous*: suspended filigree and crystal
  that inhabit the space around the body without touching it
- exception kept as inspiration only: the khalkhal / barefoot-sandal
  *composition* (ankle chain + toe ring) re-interpreted as floating
  disconnected segments

## Gold as Ornament

**Rejected because:**

- Director guardrail (2026-10-20): champagne gold exists only as a
  **thin seam line** marking the boundary between tissue and graft —
  *«l'oro deve restare una linea sottile, non un ornamento»*
- generators given «gold» drift to arabesques and filigree, which is
  exactly the pull that leads back to Elesh Norn territory

## Painted Markings on the Host

**Rejected because:**

- the kit adds pieces *on top* of the unchanged creature; painting
  marks on skin/fur breaks the legibility contract (host intact +
  kit over it) and turns mutation into decoration
- the Neoranga-style geometric marks are a *candidate* engraved into
  **ivory grafts** (gold or ruby-red grooves, TBD) — never pigment on
  living tissue
