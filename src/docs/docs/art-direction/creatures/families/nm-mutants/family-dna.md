# Nm Mutants — Family DNA

Working name «Nm Mutants» (Director: *«l'aberrazione con le Nm si possa
applicare a qualsiasi altra cosa»* / *«parliamo di mutazioni di Nm»*,
R-237, 2026-10-20). **Family of process, not of anatomy**: every member
is a host creature mutated by the **Nm** — a sci-fi mutation agent —
whose work expresses through two complementary grammars. What the
family shares is a mutation grammar, not a body plan — a mutated beast
and a mutated sapient look different, but obey the same material law.

The mutation is a **kit grafted onto the host** (Director ruling,
R-237, 2026-10-20): the original creature stays itself — fur, feathers
and natural colors intact; marks are *never painted on the body* —
while the Nm add pieces **on top**. This solves the animal-members
problem: a mutated wolf keeps its pelt.

## World Pillar

Third pole — the collision of the two existing pillars:

- **Vicissitudine** reads *Wilderness*: living matter, organic
  sculpture, transformation of flesh and bone.
- **Nm** reads *Empire*: precise metalwork, crystalline geometry,
  ornament and ceremony.

An Nm-mutated creature is where the two grammars meet on one body. This
makes the family the visible face of a possible third pole of the world
(candidate decision — pillar tag is `draft` pending Director ruling;
cosmological definition of Vicissitudine and Nm is `world`-authority,
still open — expansion of «Nm», e.g. nanomachines, unconfirmed).

## Core Fantasy

A living being re-sculpted into a higher, alien order of beauty: flesh
becomes gemstone, bone becomes ivory, ornament becomes autonomous
architecture floating around the body. The host remains legible — you
can always read *what it was* beneath *what it became*.

## The Two Processes

- **Vicissitudine — living matter**: sculpts flesh, tendon and raw
  bone. Irregular, asymmetric, alive — the organic side of the
  transformation.
- **Nm — the kit**: pieces grafted onto the unchanged host, built from
  three materials with one role each (Material Law below). Precise,
  geometric, weightless. Nm uses **empty space as a material** — an arc
  that hovers above a shoulder, a filigree line that never touches the
  skin, a chain whose segments are not connected.

The two processes never blend randomly. On a body they read as two
different aspects: living irregular tissue versus ivory, gold and
prism.

## Material Law — the Nm kit

Three materials, one role each (Director ruling, 2026-10-20):

- **Ivory = structure.** The material the Nm use to reconstruct any
  piece: plates, limbs, reinforcements. Rebuilt parts are always ivory
  even when they copy another species' anatomy — the *color* tells you
  who made it, the *shape* tells you where it was copied from.
- **Champagne gold = seam.** A thin vein along the boundary between
  original tissue and graft: it marks *where* the creature was remade.
  A line, never an ornament.
- **Prismatic = light.** Transparent veils and membranes with a
  gasoline-like iridescent reflection — reserved for everything light
  or floating.

## Legibility Rules

- **Chimerism is legible.** Grafted pieces are ivory whatever their
  shape; the host keeps its own colors. The contrast between natural
  body and kit *is* the mutation made visible.
- **Affinity reads by quantity — and by damage.** The more grafts,
  the less affinity the creature had with the Nm (established rule).
  Refined (Director ruling, 2026-10-21): the *less suited* the host,
  the harsher the work — white plates, **mutilations, tumors,
  carved tattoos and gouged prismatic wounds** cut into living
  tissue. Prismatic iridescence on low-affinity bodies is a
  *carved wound*, not a veil: the veil-only rule governs the kit;
  the afflicted flesh carries the scars of the graft.
- **Chimerism is an Nm upgrade.** When a host is too "scarce"
  (weak, unsuitable), the Nm *improve* it — sometimes by grafting
  better donor DNA (Director ruling, 2026-10-21). The donor
  vocabulary is deliberate, not random — it borrows fitness traits:
  the documented gohul carry **ichthyic/marine upgrades** (compound
  eye-clusters, sucker-pits, wet-look iridescent tissue). Read:
  the more borrowed anatomy a creature shows, the worse the
  original stock was.
- **Neoranga-style geometric marks** (candidate, TBD): engraved into
  the ivory parts, grooves filled with gold or ruby-red — deeper on
  less-affine creatures; a creature's dominant stat may tint its veil.
  Both fittings are options, not decided.

## Face Law — the remade face (Director ruling, 2026-10-21)

The face of a remade creature must carry an **accentuated congenital
problem**: it has to read immediately as a weak, sickly constitution.
At least one of the following, often more than one:

- **Congenital deformation, amplified** — receded or missing chin,
  hollow temples, asymmetric skull, underdeveloped structure: the
  face of someone who was born fragile and was *not* healed.
- **A visible external tumor** — a growth that reads on the surface,
  integrated into the anatomy, never gore.
- **An extreme facial wound** — *Shigurui* register: no chin left, a
  mouth that opens unnaturally because the cheeks were cut, a nose
  that no longer exists. The wound is dry, anatomical, settled into
  the new biology — a scar the transformation keeps, not an injury
  in progress.

On non-human hosts the same law applies to the head/muzzle: the
remade head always tells you the creature was already marked before
the Nm touched it — or that the touch itself left a wound.

**Caste exception (Director ruling, 2026-10-21):** the Face Law
binds the **non-vampire members** — gohul thralls and servants
(e.g. the Pathfinder) made from living creatures. The **vampire
caste** (human base: Regina degli Ibridi, Guardia Reale) keeps a
beautiful, intact face — their displayed wound lives elsewhere
(the queen's burned hand); they are what the transformation
*offers*, the remade wear what it *costs*.

## Mandatory Traits

- The host stays itself: natural anatomy, colors, fur or feathers
  untouched — the kit adds pieces, never paints.
- **Faces obey the Face Law** (above) — accentuated congenital
  problems, a visible tumor, or an extreme settled wound; the
  Sovereign exception applies only where ruled.
- Reconstructed pieces are always **ivory**.
- Every boundary between original tissue and graft carries a **thin
  champagne-gold seam**.
- **Prismatic iridescence lives only inside veils and membranes** —
  never diffused over solid surfaces.
- Vicissitudine-side flesh may read as gemstone-like translucent mass
  (ruby for the Sovereign); never open wound.
- Joints and small functional details may be metallic.
- Nm pieces graft or float; they are never worn, embedded like
  jewelry, or painted on.
- Resting silhouettes keep an order: organic mass low and near the
  body, kit architecture high and floating.

## Forbidden

- Jewelry embedded or worn — no gems set into flesh, no clothing, no
  costume logic.
- Industrial biomechanics: tubes, cables, gears, cybernetic implants.
- Red gore: exposed raw muscle, wounds, viscera. Muscle is ruby, not
  meat.
- Amorphous mass: goo, undifferentiated tentacles as the main feature
  (the aberration is structured — cf. desiderata v9).
- Perfect bilateral symmetry; sci-fi surfaces.
- **Gold as ornament** — arabesques, filigree, gilded decoration.
  Champagne gold exists only as thin seam lines (Director guardrail:
  *«l'oro deve restare una linea sottile, non un ornamento»* — "gold"
  in a prompt drags generators toward filigree and back to Elesh
  Norn).
- **Diffuse prismatic sheen** on solid surfaces — iridescence stays
  confined to precise veils and membranes, otherwise it spreads
  everywhere.
- **Painted markings** on skin, fur or feathers — the kit adds pieces
  on top, never paints the host.
- The Elesh Norn formula taken whole: blade-crown + white organic
  armor + red exposed flesh (see rejected-directions).

## Emotional Space

- Awe
- Unsettling beauty
- Reverence with unease — never disgust, never plain horror

Wanderlust tone is preserved: wonder before revulsion. The ruby-flesh
and ivory-bone material law exists precisely to keep anatomy out of
gore territory (Director ruling, R-237, 2026-10-20).

## Evolution Grammar (stages of aberration)

- **Stage I — Touched**: host nearly intact; first Vicissitudine
  growths (a bony spur, a reshaped limb) and a few Nm filaments.
- **Stage II — Woven**: functional transformation — locomotion, limbs
  or profile visibly reworked; a coherent Nm ornament structure
  floats around the body.
- **Stage III — Sovereign**: anatomy fully re-sculpted into an icon;
  the ornament architecture is a court/halo (see the Regina degli
  Ibridi). The host species is still readable underneath.

Sister lineages may diverge by dominance: *Nm-dominant* mutants
(geometric, cold, ornate) vs *Vicissitudine-dominant* mutants
(proliferating organic sculpture). Declared per-member via
`aberration_stage` + dominance + graft density (the visible affinity
index) (model C, R-237).

## Material Palette

- **Kit (Nm):** noble ivory (structure), champagne gold (seams only),
  prismatic gasoline-iridescence (veils and membranes only) —
  literally the Prismatic of Wanderlust surfacing as light.
- **Vicissitudine / host organic:** living, irregular tissue — flesh,
  tendon, raw bone; sculpted organic may read as gemstone translucency
  (ruby for the Sovereign).
- **Forbidden:** chrome, steel, plastic, rubber, raw red flesh,
  ornamental gold, painted markings.

## Visual Inspiration

- **H.R. Giger** — organic-mechanical fusion (take the synthesis,
  refuse the industrial tubes)
- **Elesh Norn / Mother of Machines (MTG)** — hieratic transformed
  humanoid; anti-reference for crown/armor/gore (see rejected)
- **René Lalique / Art Nouveau jewelry** — organic glass-and-metal
  ornament as living filigree (the Nm grammar)
- **Arabic filigree & khalkhal / barefoot sandals** — suspended chain
  ornament, decoration that never touches skin
- **Jellyfish (medusa)** — float grammar for Nm structures: liquid,
  delayed, self-suspended
- **Cephalaspis and prehistoric armored fish** — backward-swept
  cranial shield geometry (extends behind the head, never laterally)
- **Mother-of-pearl / nacre** — prismatic underlayer material
- **Scrimshaw and ivory carving** — bone as noble, carved material
- **Mucha** — ornamental curve discipline around the figure
- **Shigurui (anime)** — register for extreme settled facial wounds:
  missing chin, mouth opened by cut cheeks, absent nose — anatomy
  kept dry and designed, never gore (Face Law)

### Caste structure (Director ruling, 2026-10-21)

- **Gohul** (Director's spelling of the vampire-lore *ghoul*): the
  low-rank servant caste — made from **any living creature** as
  host (not vampires): a gohul is a ghoul thrall — the host body
  broken and reworked. Rough surfaces, Face Law fully applied;
  low-affinity damage vocabulary (plates, mutilations, tumors,
  carved tattoos, gouged prismatic wounds) and zoned chimerism.
- **Vampire caste** (Regina degli Ibridi, Guardia Reale): base
  **human + vampire** — the kit executed clean; the face is kept
  (Face Law exception applies to vampire rank — confirmed by the
  guard reference 2026-10-21; formal family-level ratification
  still pending Director).

Emerging rank ladder by kit completeness: Queen (sovereign icon) >
Guardia Reale (clean elite) > Pathfinder (rough heavy graft) >
Gohul (broken thralls).

Mutated forms of other families' creatures are declared on the host
creature card via `host_family` + `aberration_stage` (model C): this
family owns the mutation grammar; the member owns the host anatomy.
