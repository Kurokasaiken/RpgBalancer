# Mythic Beasts — Rejected Directions

## Wyvern

**Rejected because:**

- too generic western dragon
- lacks iridescent/emerald material specificity
- silhouette not distinct enough

## Dinosaur Reskin

**Rejected because:**

- too biological
- breaks mythic/ceremonial tone
- feels prehistoric rather than legendary

## Feathered Serpent God

**Rejected because:**

- too close to specific real-world deity
- limits creative freedom
- risks cultural appropriation
