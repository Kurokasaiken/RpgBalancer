# Mythic Beasts — Family DNA

## World Pillar

Wilderness — ancient jungles, forgotten ruins, sun-drenched peaks.

## Core Fantasy

Ancient animals made mythic by scale, material, and stillness.

## Mandatory Traits

- Animal base recognizable
- Monumental or ancient material overlay
- Asymmetric proportions
- Slow majesty or sudden predator burst
- Presence changes local environment

## Forbidden

- Humanoid anatomy
- Armor as separate object
- Sci-fi tech
- Cartoonish proportions
- Perfect symmetry

## Emotional Space

- Awe
- Reverence
- Smallness of the player

## Evolution Grammar

- **Stage I:** hidden hatchling or fragment
- **Stage II:** guardian beast or predator
- **Stage III:** world-legend / titan

## Material Palette

- **Primary:** iridescent scales, polished gold, marble, emerald
- **Secondary:** tropical foliage, antique bronze, sun-bleached bone
- **Forbidden:** steel, plastic, neon, chrome

## Visual Inspiration

- **Creature Design:** Hayao Miyazaki dragons, Hindu/Buddhist serpent deities
- **Material:** polished jade and gold temple sculpture
- **Movement:** slow coiling, sudden lunge, majestic stillness
