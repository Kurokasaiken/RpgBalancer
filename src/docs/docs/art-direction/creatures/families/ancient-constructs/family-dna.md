# Ancient Constructs — Family DNA

## World Pillar

Empire — basalt deserts, colossal suspended architecture, ritual imperial machinery.

## Core Fantasy

Sleeping baroque machines and statues that wake only when ceremony is broken.

## Mandatory Traits

- Heavy baroque bronze, stone, or basalt materials
- Inertia and slow deliberate movement
- Runic or ceremonial carvings
- Asymmetric plates and joints
- Wake/sleep state transitions

## Forbidden

- Sci-fi wires, pipes, exoskeletons
- Humanoid faces and expressions
- Perfect symmetry
- Modern metals (steel, chrome)
- Flat digital surfaces

## Emotional Space

- Awe
- Reverence
- Dread of interruption

## Evolution Grammar

- **Stage I:** dormant fragment or echo
- **Stage II:** active guardian or relic
- **Stage III:** colossus or sun-crowned titan

## Material Palette

- **Primary:** baroque bronze, black basalt, sun-bronze
- **Secondary:** runic gold, ritual oil, polished obsidian
- **Forbidden:** steel, chrome, plastic, rubber

## Visual Inspiration

- **Creature Design:** ancient cathedral machinery, baroque automata, sleeping Buddhas
- **Material:** oxidized bronze sculpture, cracked marble, ritual oil
- **Movement:** stop-motion inertia, mechanical wake-up, vibrating runes
