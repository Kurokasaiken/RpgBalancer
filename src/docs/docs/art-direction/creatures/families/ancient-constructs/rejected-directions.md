# Ancient Constructs — Rejected Directions

## Robot Golem

**Rejected because:**

- too sci-fi
- wires and pistons violate family material palette
- breaks imperial/ritual fantasy

## Animated Armor

**Rejected because:**

- too close to standard fantasy trope
- lacks sleep/wake ritual logic
- weak silhouette without runic presence

## Stone Guardian

**Rejected because:**

- too generic
- lacks bronze baroque weight
- not distinct enough from classical golem
